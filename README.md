# Threat Intelligence Report: Multi-Stage Tenant Compromise & Fraud Chain

**Status:** Published  
**Severity:** High  
**Target Industry:** Corporate / Enterprise Tenants  
**Vector:** Teams Direct Routing & Typosquatting  

---

## Executive Summary

This report details an observed multi-stage attack campaign originating from a compromised corporate Microsoft Teams tenant. The threat actor leverages deep-linked Teams Direct Routing to bypass initial security controls, pivoting from financial fraud attempts to spoofed federal infrastructure for advanced credential harvesting and device fingerprinting.
